# Glossary

Plain-language definitions for the terms you'll meet at the vet and in this guide.

AAFCO
:   The Association of American Feed Control Officials. A food's **AAFCO statement** tells you it's *complete and balanced* for a life stage (e.g., "growth" for puppies). Look for it on the bag.

AAHA
:   American Animal Hospital Association — sets voluntary U.S. standards for veterinary hospitals and publishes the canine **vaccination** and **life-stage** guidelines this guide follows.

Body condition score (BCS)
:   A 1–9 (or 1–5) scale vets use to judge whether a dog is too thin, ideal, or overweight — based on feeling ribs and seeing a waist, not just weight.

Booster
:   A repeat vaccine dose that strengthens and extends immunity after the initial series.

Capillary refill time (CRT)
:   Press a gum, release, and count how long color takes to return (~2 seconds is normal). A quick at-home check of circulation.

Core vs. non-core vaccine
:   **Core** = recommended for every dog (DAPP/DHPP, leptospirosis, rabies). **Non-core** = chosen by lifestyle/region (Bordetella, Lyme, canine influenza).

DAPP / DHPP / DA2PP
:   The core combination vaccine: **D**istemper, **A**denovirus/**H**epatitis, **P**arainfluenza, **P**arvovirus. Given as a puppy series.

Deciduous (baby) teeth
:   A puppy's first set of 28 teeth, replaced by 42 adult teeth by ~6–7 months. **Retained** baby teeth sometimes need removal.

Dewclaw
:   The "thumb" claw higher up on the inside of the leg; needs trimming too.

Enzymatic cleaner
:   A cleaner that breaks down the odor compounds in urine/feces so dogs don't return to re-soil the spot — unlike plain soap.

GDV / Bloat
:   Gastric dilatation-volvulus — a life-threatening twisting of the stomach, most common in large, deep-chested breeds. A true [emergency](../05-health/emergencies.md#bloat).

Heartworm
:   A mosquito-borne parasite that's serious to treat but easy to **prevent** with year-round monthly medication.

Hypoglycemia
:   Low blood sugar — a particular risk in **toy-breed** puppies, causing weakness, trembling, or collapse.

Microchip
:   A tiny implanted ID, scannable at vets and shelters, that links to your contact info in a registry — your best chance of reunion if your dog is lost.

Parvovirus (parvo)
:   A highly contagious, potentially fatal virus dangerous to under-vaccinated puppies; the reason we complete the vaccine series before risky outings.

Positive reinforcement
:   Training by rewarding desired behavior (rather than punishing unwanted behavior) — the evidence-backed, vet-recommended approach.

Pyometra
:   A serious, often life-threatening uterine infection in unspayed females — prevented by spaying.

RER / MER
:   **Resting** and **Maintenance** Energy Requirements — the calorie formulas behind the [feeding calculator](feeding-calculator.md).

Skeletal maturity
:   When the growth plates close and bones stop lengthening — later in big dogs, and the key timing factor for large-breed [spay/neuter](../04-growing-up/spay-neuter.md) and high-impact exercise.

Socialization window
:   The ~3–14-week period when puppies most readily accept new experiences as normal. See [Socializing safely](../03-foundations/socialization.md).

Spay / neuter
:   Surgical sterilization of females (spay) and males (neuter). Timing is increasingly **size- and breed-specific**.

Zoonotic
:   A disease that can spread between animals and people (e.g., rabies, leptospirosis, roundworms) — a reason for vaccination and good hygiene.
