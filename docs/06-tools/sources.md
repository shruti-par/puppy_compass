# Sources & further reading

This guide reflects current U.S. veterinary guidance as of mid-2026. It's educational, not a substitute for your own veterinarian. Below are the kinds of authoritative sources the recommendations draw on, and where to go deeper.

## Primary guidance the guide follows

- **American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA)** — *Canine Vaccination Guidelines* (2022, updated 2024 to make **leptospirosis core** for all dogs) and the *Canine Life Stage Guidelines*, which inform the size-based spay/neuter timing. [aaha.org](https://www.aaha.org)
- **American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA)** — general pet-owner health and safety resources. [avma.org](https://www.avma.org)
- **WSAVA** — global vaccination guidelines (2024) and spay/neuter guidance, useful context alongside the U.S. recommendations. [wsava.org](https://wsava.org)
- **ASPCA Animal Poison Control** — the toxic-foods and toxic-plants lists, and the **(888) 426-4435** poison hotline. [aspca.org](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control)
- **American Kennel Club (AKC)** — breed information, growth/development, and reporting on the 2024 UC Davis spay/neuter research. [akc.org](https://www.akc.org)
- **UC Davis School of Veterinary Medicine** — breed-specific spay/neuter timing research (the 2013 study and its 2024 expansion, published in *Frontiers in Veterinary Science*), behind the large-breed "wait for maturity" guidance.
- **Companion Animal Parasite Council (CAPC)** — deworming and year-round parasite-prevention cadence. [capcvet.org](https://capcvet.org)

## Trusted places to read more

- **Veterinary Partner (VIN)** — vet-written, plain-language articles on almost any condition. [veterinarypartner.vin.com](https://veterinarypartner.vin.com)
- **AAHA pet-owner resources** and your **own clinic's** handouts.
- **Fear Free / certified positive-reinforcement trainers** — for training and behavior; for serious behavior concerns, a **board-certified veterinary behaviorist (DACVB)**.

## A note on how to weigh advice

The internet is full of confident, contradictory puppy advice. A few filters that help:

1. **Prefer veterinary and academic sources** over forums and influencers.
2. **Check the date** — guidance genuinely changes (vaccines and spay/neuter timing both have in recent years).
3. **Individualize.** Your vet's advice for *your* dog beats any general rule here, including ours.

!!! note "Keeping this guide current"
    Veterinary recommendations evolve. If you're reading this well after
    mid-2026, double-check vaccine schedules and spay/neuter timing against the
    current AAHA/AVMA guidance and your vet — and update the relevant pages.
