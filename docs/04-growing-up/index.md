# Growing up

<span class="pc-stage">:material-dog-side: Stage: roughly 4–18 months</span>

The tiny, sleepy potato you brought home is becoming a real dog — with opinions. This section covers the longer arc:

1. **[Growth stages](growth-stages.md)** — what's happening physically and mentally, and how the timeline differs wildly by size.
2. **[Surviving adolescence](adolescence.md)** — the "teenage" months when your trained puppy suddenly forgets everything. (It's temporary. Promise.)
3. **[Spay & neuter timing](spay-neuter.md)** — one of the few decisions where the *timing* genuinely matters for long-term health, and where the right answer is now size- and breed-specific.

!!! tip "The big theme of this stage"
    **Maturity is not the same as size.** A large-breed dog can look fully
    grown at 7 months and still be an impulsive, growth-plate-open adolescent
    underneath. Match your expectations — and decisions like exercise intensity
    and surgery timing — to *developmental* age, not appearance.
