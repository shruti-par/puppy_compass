# Welcome — you've got this

## How this guide thinks about advice

Puppies are not interchangeable. A 4-pound Chihuahua and a 60-pound Labrador puppy have different bones, different stomachs, different timelines, and different risks. So wherever it matters, the advice here is **size-, breed-, and age-specific** rather than one-size-fits-all. You'll see callouts like this throughout:

!!! tip "Size note"
    **Small/toy breeds** (under ~25 lb adult) mature fast and chill easily.
    **Large/giant breeds** (over ~50 lb adult) grow for much longer, which
    changes feeding, exercise, and surgery timing. We'll flag the difference
    every time it's relevant.

## A promise and a disclaimer

The promise: by the end of this, you'll feel like you know what you're doing — because you will.

The disclaimer, because it's important: **this is educational content, not veterinary advice.** It reflects current U.S. guidance from sources like the AAHA, AVMA, ASPCA, and AKC, but your own veterinarian knows *your* puppy and *your* region. When the two ever disagree, follow your vet. See [Sources & further reading](06-tools/sources.md) for where this guidance comes from.
