# Foundations

<span class="pc-stage">:material-school-outline: Stage: roughly 8 weeks–4 months (and ongoing)</span>

If the first weeks were about safety and routine, foundations are about shaping the **adult dog your puppy will become.** Three things matter most here, and one of them is on a strict clock:

1. **[Socializing safely](socialization.md)** — the most time-sensitive thing in this whole guide. The key window closes around 14–16 weeks.
2. **[Training the basics](training.md)** — the manners and skills that make life together easy.
3. **[Daily grooming & hygiene](grooming.md)** — a routine for coat, teeth, and nails that you start now so it's normal forever.

!!! danger "Read the socialization chapter early"
    You can teach "sit" at any age. You **cannot** re-open the prime
    socialization window once it closes. If you only deeply read one chapter in
    this guide while your puppy is young, make it
    [Socializing safely](socialization.md).

These run alongside everything from the previous section — keep up the feeding, potty, and crate routines as you add these.
