# The first weeks

<span class="pc-stage">:material-baby-bottle-outline: Stage: roughly 8–16 weeks old</span>

This is the deep end. Your puppy is home, everything is new and a little chaotic, and you're running on less sleep than you'd like. Good news: the things that matter most in these weeks are simple, and they compound. Get feeding, vaccines, potty, crate, and gentle routine right, and almost everything else gets easier.

Work through these roughly in order:

1. **[The first 72 hours](first-72-hours.md)** — settle in without overwhelming them.
2. **[Good vs. bad](good-and-bad.md)** — the foods, plants, and hazards to know cold.
3. **[Feeding](feeding.md)** — how much, what, and how often (with a calculator).
4. **[The first vet visit](first-vet-visit.md)** — the medical baseline.
5. **[Vaccines & deworming](vaccines.md)** — the schedule that keeps them safe.
6. **[Potty training](potty-training.md)** — consistency beats everything.
7. **[Crate training](crate-training.md)** — a safe den, and the key to sleep.

!!! tip "The one mindset shift for these weeks"
    You are not "obedience training" yet — you're **building trust and
    routine.** Predictable food, potty, nap, and play cycles do more for a
    young puppy than any command. Calm and boring is a feature, not a bug.

Running in parallel, the clock is already ticking on the [socialization window](../03-foundations/socialization.md) — read that chapter early, because the most important developmental period closes around 14–16 weeks.
