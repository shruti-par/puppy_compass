# How to use this guide

You can absolutely read this front to back — it's written to flow that way. But most people dip in and out, so here's how to get what you need fast.

## Read it like a timeline

The chapters are ordered the way your puppy's life unfolds. If your puppy is:

| Your puppy is… | Start around here |
| --- | --- |
| Not home yet | [Before Home](../01-before-home/index.md) — proof the house, shop, pick a vet |
| 8–10 weeks (just arrived) | [The first 72 hours](../02-first-weeks/first-72-hours.md) |
| 8–16 weeks | [Vaccines](../02-first-weeks/vaccines.md), [potty](../02-first-weeks/potty-training.md) & [crate training](../02-first-weeks/crate-training.md), and the all-important [socialization window](../03-foundations/socialization.md) |
| 4–6 months | [Training the basics](../03-foundations/training.md), [daily grooming](../03-foundations/grooming.md), teething |
| 6–18 months | [Adolescence](../04-growing-up/adolescence.md) and [spay/neuter timing](../04-growing-up/spay-neuter.md) |
| Any age, feeling unwell | [Is this normal?](../05-health/spotting-illness.md) and [Emergencies](../05-health/emergencies.md) |

## Use the search

Press ++slash++ (or tap the magnifying glass) and type — "diarrhea," "biting," "crate at night," "when to neuter a Lab." The whole guide is indexed, so search is usually faster than browsing.

## The tools do the math for you

Two chapters are interactive:

- The **[Feeding calculator](../06-tools/feeding-calculator.md)** turns your puppy's weight and age into a daily calorie and cup estimate.
- The **[Vaccine & deworming planner](../06-tools/vaccine-planner.md)** turns a birth date into a dated schedule you can take to your vet.

There are also **[printable checklists](../06-tools/checklists.md)** for shopping, puppy-proofing, and the first vet visit.

## What the callout boxes mean

!!! note "Note — good to know"
    Helpful context that's nice to have.

!!! tip "Tip / Size note — do this"
    A recommendation, often size- or breed-specific.

!!! warning "Warning — be careful"
    Common mistakes and things that can hurt your puppy.

!!! danger "Danger — act now"
    Safety-critical. Don't scroll past these.

One last thing: **go at your puppy's pace, not the internet's.** Comparison is the thief of puppy-raising joy. Your puppy doesn't read milestone charts. Neither should you stress about them.
