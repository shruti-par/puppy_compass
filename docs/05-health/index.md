# Health & safety

<span class="pc-stage">:material-medical-bag: Reference: useful at every age</span>

This section isn't tied to a stage — it's the part you'll come back to whenever something seems off. Three chapters:

1. **[Is this normal? Spotting illness](spotting-illness.md)** — learn your puppy's baseline so you can tell ordinary puppy weirdness from a real problem.
2. **[Emergencies — what to do](emergencies.md)** — the conditions that mean "go now," and the calm steps to take.
3. **[Parasites & prevention](parasites.md)** — worms, fleas, ticks, and heartworm, and the simple routine that prevents them.

!!! danger "In an emergency, skip straight to the action"
    Don't read a whole page mid-crisis. Use the
    [Emergency Quick Reference](../00-start/emergency-card.md) card and call your
    vet, the nearest 24/7 ER, or poison control.

The single most valuable health skill you can build is simply **knowing what's normal for your puppy** — appetite, energy, gums, stool, breathing. Everything else is noticing when that changes. Start there.
