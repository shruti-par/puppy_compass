# Before your puppy comes home

<span class="pc-stage">:material-clock-outline: Stage: the week or two before pickup</span>

The work you do *before* your puppy arrives is the work you'll be most grateful for later. A puppy-proofed house and a stocked supply closet mean that on day one you can focus on the only thing that matters — helping a small, confused animal feel safe.

This section is three jobs, in order:

1. **[Puppy-proof your home](puppy-proofing.md)** — get down on the floor and see the world the way a curious mouth will.
2. **[Buy the essentials](essentials.md)** — what you actually need on day one vs. what can wait (with a size-specific shopping list).
3. **[Choose a vet](choosing-a-vet.md)** — ideally lined up *before* pickup, so the first-week check-up is easy to book.

!!! tip "Do this first"
    Book your puppy's **first vet visit for within 48–72 hours of bringing
    them home**, before you even pick them up if you can. Many breeders and
    rescues actually require a vet check within a few days as part of their
    health guarantee.

Take it one page at a time. None of this needs to be perfect — it needs to be done.
