# The essentials shopping list

You do not need the entire pet store. You need a focused day-one kit, plus a few things you'll grow into. Here's what actually earns its place, what to size correctly, and what can wait.

## Sizing the things that matter

!!! tip "Crate sizing — the one people get wrong"
    The crate should be **just big enough to stand up, turn around, and lie
    down** — no bigger, or a puppy will potty in one corner and sleep in the
    other. For a **large-breed puppy**, buy the full adult-size crate and use a
    **divider** to shrink it now and expand it as they grow. For a **toy
    breed**, a small crate is plenty for life.

!!! tip "Collar & harness sizing"
    You should fit **two fingers** under a collar. Puppies outgrow collars
    fast — expect to re-buy. For walks, a **front- or back-clip Y-harness**
    avoids pressure on the throat; skip choke, prong, and "no-pull" tightening
    harnesses on a puppy.

## Toys: what's safe, what's not

- **Good:** rubber chew toys you can dent with a fingernail, puzzle/food-dispensing toys, and appropriately sized plush toys (supervised).
- **The "two rules" for chew hardness:** if you can't make a dent with your thumbnail, and if it would hurt to be hit on the knee with it, it's too hard and can fracture teeth. That rules out antlers, hard nylon bones, hooves, and real bones for most puppies.
- **Avoid:** anything small enough to swallow, toys with button eyes or squeakers a strong chewer can extract, cooked bones, and rawhide for heavy chewers (chunks can cause blockages).

## Nice-to-have (can wait a week or two)

- Grooming kit: a brush suited to the coat, **dog-safe nail clippers or a grinder**, dog toothbrush + **dog** toothpaste (never human paste).
- A **seatbelt harness or crash-tested carrier** for car trips.
- A **long line** (15–30 ft) for later recall practice.
- Pet insurance — worth pricing out in the first couple of weeks, before any condition becomes "pre-existing."

## Budgeting honestly

First-month costs add up: supplies, the first vet visit, the vaccine series, and likely microchipping and the spay/neuter fund later. Build a small buffer for the unexpected — puppies eat things. The interactive **[shopping checklist](../06-tools/checklists.md)** version of this list is printable.
